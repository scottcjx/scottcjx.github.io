Eduboat began as my 2025 hackathon project and was subsequently revisited through two further iterations with Mecatron. The project remains ongoing.

I designed and led a team to develop and deliver multiple Eduboat workshops. The sessions introduce engineering through boat design and construction for students from secondary school through junior college.

The project combines hands-on making with instruction. Participants work through design, prototyping, testing, and iteration while building a working boat. I shape the workshop format, coordinate the team, and lead delivery across multiple sessions.
