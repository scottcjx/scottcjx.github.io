As Lead of Software and Electrical Systems for Nanyang Venture Formula Racing, I designed and assembled custom PCBs for throttle and brake sensors, the dashboard, safety-stop systems, and the main vehicle control unit. I also served as Electrical Safety Officer and taught courses in embedded programming, PCB design, and manufacturing.
