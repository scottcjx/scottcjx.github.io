I am a Computer Science undergraduate at [Nanyang Technological University](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/), where I am also pursuing a second major in Entrepreneurship. I recently spent an exchange semester at [Queen's University](https://www.queensu.ca/) studying artificial intelligence, machine learning, human–computer interaction, and graduate-level networking.

My work spans software, data, robotics, electrical systems, communications, and research. As part of my final-year project at NTU, I am a part-time research intern at the Acoustics Research Lab at the [National University of Singapore](https://arl.nus.edu.sg/). My thesis, *Integrated Communications, Sensing, and Propulsion (ICSP)*, investigates the use of thrusters as transmitters.

Previously, I worked on neural electrophysiology systems at [Axoft](https://axoft.us/), autonomous ground vehicles at Singapore's [Government Technology Agency](https://www.tech.gov.sg/), and vehicle electronics and embedded systems with [Nanyang Venture Formula Racing](https://www.instagram.com/ntuformularacing/). I also lead systems engineering and team operations at [Mecatron](https://www.mecatron.sg).

As Head of Mecatron, I coordinate work across all teams while contributing directly to networking, electrical-systems architecture and implementation, power systems, communications, and PCB design. I also teach external engineering workshops such as [Eduboat](/projects/eduboat-workshops/), run technical courses within Mecatron and Nanyang Venture Formula Racing, and contribute to research, business, finance, logistics, operations, and external relations.

My research interests include underwater acoustic networking and localization, robotics, wireless communication, marine systems, prosthetics, and neurotechnology. More broadly, I am interested in integrating sensing, communication, and control into systems that operate in difficult environments.
