# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Peers and researchers exploring Scott Cheng Jia Xu's projects, publications,
education, notes, and research-adjacent work.

## Product Purpose

A personal academic profile that makes Scott's body of work easy to understand,
browse, and revisit. Success means a visitor can quickly find the work most
relevant to them, understand the themes connecting it, and follow through to a
project, publication, or contact point.

## Positioning

The portfolio is a living academic profile of one person's ideas and outputs,
not a generic resume, sales page, or academic database. The relationships
between projects, writing, publications, and research interests are part of the
experience.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from search, a shared link, or a citation and browse on phones
and laptops. Content is published as Markdown, YAML, and JSON in a static Astro
site hosted free on GitHub Pages.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The site must build as a static GitHub Pages site with no paid services or server-side runtime. Content should remain easy to modify through Markdown/YAML data files. Existing content collections and useful URLs should remain available unless the new structure replaces them cleanly.

## Brand Commitments

The site uses Scott Cheng as its displayed name and preserves the existing real
content, profile image, publications, projects, and links. Do not invent
testimonials, clients, metrics, or research claims.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing content collections in `_posts`, `_publications`, `_talks`, `_teaching`, and `_portfolio`; profile and CV data in `_data`; assets in `images/` and `files/`.

## Product Principles

- Let the work lead from the first viewport.
- Make browsing relationships feel intentional, not database-like.
- Keep publishing as easy as editing Markdown, YAML, or JSON.
- Prefer honest context over generic personal-brand claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, responsive
layouts, reduced-motion fallbacks, and a usable light/dark theme toggle.
